## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an upscale, hospitality-inspired corporate travel and expense ecosystem. It combines the editorial authority of high-end business travel publications with the rigorous clarity required for enterprise fiscal workflows and corporate itinerary management.

### Personality & Tone

- **Authoritative yet Gracious:** Balances fiscal compliance and policy rigor with the warmth of a luxury concierge.
- **Editorial Executive:** Replaces sterile, monochromatic software tables with considered typography, generous whitespace, and balanced surface contrast.
- **Calm & Discerning:** Uses muted marine blues, warm parchment backgrounds, and maritime teals to lower anxiety around travel delays, booking discrepancies, and budget allocations.

### Design Movement: Warm Editorial Minimalism

The visual language merges modern digital utility with classic editorial design:

- **Canvas Distinction:** The root canvas uses a warm linen neutral rather than stark clinical white, making prolonged dashboard sessions easier on executive eyes.
- **Typographic Contrast:** High-character serif titles evoke premium editorial publications, while high-legibility sans-serif metrics keep data dense and scan-friendly.
- **Signature Vignettes:** High-contrast dark navy hero banners provide anchor points across workspaces, accented with pill controls and chromatic navy shadows.

## Colors

The palette is derived from classic nautical corporate tones: deep naval slates, sea-spray teals, mist blues, and warm linen neutrals.

### Palette Hierarchy & Application

- **Primary (`#2F4156` Navy):** Main visual anchor. Powers primary interactive buttons, standard body typography, strong outlines, and deep contextual icons.
- **Secondary (`#567C8D` Teal):** Brand accent and interactive indicator. Used for selected states, hyperlinked actions, active navigational tabs, and gradient stops.
- **Tertiary (`#C8D9E6` Sky Blue):** Light tint accent used for graphic focal points, subtle decorative gradients, progress bar tracks, and brand identity marks.
- **Neutral (`#F5EFEB` Linen / Beige):** Warm structural background tint. Used to soften surface transitions, create contrast within dark button text, and shade secondary modules.
- **Canvas Base (`#EFEAE3`):** Warm, grounded canvas background that separates white card panels without harsh grey dropouts.
- **Navy Deep (`#1E2937`):** High-density dark shade for top-tier headings, hero greeting panels, and strong tabular numerical values.

### Semantic Status Badges

Status indicators use balanced pastel containers paired with high-contrast text:

- **Approved / Settled (`--ok`):** `#E4EFE7` container with `#3D7A56` text.
- **Pending / In Review (`--pend`):** `#FBF0DD` container with `#9B7226` text.
- **Scheduled / Itinerary Ready (`--sky`):** `#DCE9F2` container with `#2C5670` text.
- **Rejected / Over Budget (`--bad`):** `#F3E1DA` container with `#8B4A32` text.
- **Alert Indicator:** `#E8825C` notification pip with a `2px #1E2937` knockout ring.

## Typography

The typographical strategy uses **Newsreader** for primary titles and key metric counters alongside **Inter** for data points, structural controls, and tabular reports.

### Typographic Hierarchy

- **Editorial Serifs (Newsreader):** Used exclusively for context-setting landmarks: workspace greetings, card section headers, and key financial summaries. Gives dashboard interfaces a refined, publication-quality finish without slowing data scanning.
- **Functional Grotesque (Inter):** Handles all high-density tabular reads, monetary breakdowns, form inputs, button actions, and system metadata.
- **Supertitles & Column Headers:** Rendered uppercase with letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) at sizes between `11px` and `12px` to establish structural order above content cards.
- **Tabular Figures:** All financial rows, booking IDs, dates, and metric widgets must use proportional or tabular sizing to keep flight logs, ticket receipts, and balances vertically aligned.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a structured sidebar-and-canvas system that organizes dense executive workflows into clean, digestible visual modules.

### Grid & Layout Geometry

- **App Shell:** Fixed left navigation spine (`260px` wide) paired with a fluid main container (`.app-main`) padded at `34px 42px 60px`.
- **Top Greeting Panel:** Full-width hero banner spanning the main column, padded at `28px 32px` with a `32px` bottom margin to establish visual hierarchy.
- **Analytical KPI Grid (`.stat-grid`):** 4-column balanced grid with an `18px` gap, reflowing to 2 columns on tablets.
- **Operational Splits (`.two-col`):** Asymmetrical 1.6fr to 1fr layout (e.g., flight manifests alongside expense policies) separated by a `20px` gutter.
- **Action Grids (`.mini-grid`):** 4-column cluster cards separated by `16px` gutters for quick approvals, trip receipts, and travel card limits.

### Breakpoints & Adaptability

- **Desktop (>1100px):** Full desktop view with persistent `260px` sidebar, 4-column stat tiers, and split cards.
- **Tablet (821px - 1100px):** Stat grids collapse to 2 columns (`repeat(2, 1fr)`). Split grids stack vertically (`1fr`).
- **Mobile (<=820px):** Left navigation collapses behind a floating mobile drawer. Main canvas padding contracts to `16px`. Tables convert to swipeable card collections.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows and flat borders in favor of deep, colored ambient shadows and tinted surface boundaries.

### Ambient Navy Shadows

Rather than neutral black or grey shadows, all elevation draws from the base Navy palette (`rgba(47, 65, 86, ...)`), blending naturally into the linen canvas (`#EFEAE3`).

- **Surface Card Shadow (`var(--shadow-soft)`):**
  `0 24px 46px -30px rgba(47, 65, 86, 0.4)`
  Provides lifting for primary white content cards, stat containers, and data views.
- **Compact Floating Card (`var(--shadow-softer)`):**
  `0 16px 32px -24px rgba(47, 65, 86, 0.35)`
  Provides gentle depth for sub-cards and secondary modules.
- **Dark Topbar Anchor Shadow:**
  `0 30px 60px -30px rgba(30, 41, 55, 0.6)`
  Gives hero banners visual prominence over the light workspace.
- **Interactive Button Shadows:**
  - Teal Actions: `0 14px 26px -14px rgba(86, 124, 141, 0.65)`
  - Navy Actions: `0 14px 26px -14px rgba(30, 41, 55, 0.7)`

### Surface Layering & Frosted Glass

- **Hairline Dividers:** Light mode rows, inputs, and section boundaries use a soft border (`rgba(47, 65, 86, 0.08)`).
- **Dark Surface Boundaries:** Controls inside dark banners use translucent white borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.22)`).
- **Sticky Glass Elements:** Floating operational bars use an opaque base with frosted backing (`backdrop-filter: blur(10px)`).

## Shapes

The design system pairs rounded structural cards with fully pill-shaped interactive controls.

### Shape Scale & Rules

- **Interactive Controls (Pill: `999px`):** All actionable items—buttons, filter tags, navigation tabs, search inputs, and progress bars—use full pill rounding.
- **Content Surfaces (`26px`):** Primary white dashboard cards feature relaxed, modern corners that contrast against the pill-shaped controls inside them.
- **Metric Cards (`20px`):** Stat summary widgets and executive KPI cards use slightly tighter corner radiuses.
- **Form Fields & Inputs (`12px`):** Text boxes, dropdown menus, and text areas use subtle rounding to maintain structured data entry alignment.
- **Status Badges (`100px`):** Tags, state labels, and role pills maintain continuous, fully curved contours.
- **Avatars & Visual Dots (`50%`):** Profile avatars, circular icons, and alert notifications use full circles.

## Components

### 1. Buttons

- **Primary Action (Teal Gradient):** Fully pill-shaped (`border-radius: 999px`), padding `12px 22px`. Styled with a `linear-gradient(150deg, #7196A5, #567C8D)`, white text, weight 600, and a teal ambient shadow.
- **Dark Action (Navy Gradient):** Styled with `linear-gradient(160deg, #33465C, #1B242F)`, linen text (`#F5EFEB`), and a navy shadow.
- **Secondary Outline Button:** Clean transparent background with `1.5px solid #2F4156` border and `#2F4156` text.
- **Small Button Variant (`.btn-sm`):** Scaled padding `8px 15px`, font size `12px`.

### 2. Chips & Filter Pills

- **Standard Filter Pill:** White surface, hairline border (`rgba(47, 65, 86, 0.08)`), navy text (`#2F4156`), font size `12.5px`, weight 600, padding `8px 17px`.
- **Active State:** Deep navy fill (`#1E2937`), linen text (`#F5EFEB`), borderless.
- **Role Switcher Pill:** Inside dark headers, inactive state features `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)` border with `#C9D3DA` text; active state transitions to teal gradient fill with pure white typography.

### 3. Stat Cards & KPI Summaries

- **Surface Construction:** Pure white background, `20px` radius, padded at `22px 22px 20px`, elevated with `var(--shadow-soft)`.
- **Flourish Accent:** Features a circular graphic in the top right (`::before` pseudo-element) with a subtle diagonal gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #C8D9E6, #7196A5)`).
- **Content Hierarchy:** Category label in muted slate (`12px`), followed by the primary metric in serif typography (`25px`, weight 500, `#1E2937`), and a trend label with semantic coloring below.

### 4. Tabular Data Patterns

- **Header Structure:** Borderless or faint hairline bottom stroke, `0 12px 12px` cell padding, font size `11px`, weight 600, uppercase with `0.04em` tracking in muted slate (`#71808C`).
- **Body Rows:** Height balanced with `14px 12px` cell padding and `1px solid rgba(47, 65, 86, 0.08)` border. Alternate striping is avoided in favor of subtle hover transitions.
- **Route Cells:** Display origin and destination codes separated by an SVG arrow (`12px`, stroke width 2.0).

### 5. Input Fields & Dropdowns

- **Background & Border:** Input surfaces use warm off-white (`#FCFAF7`) with `1px solid rgba(47, 65, 86, 0.16)` borders and `12px` corner radiuses.
- **Sizing:** Padded at `11px 14px`, body text `13px` in deep navy (`#1E2937`), and labels in bold Inter (`12.5px`, weight 600).
- **Focus Ring:** Clean teal outline (`2px solid #567C8D`) without default browser rings.

### 6. Badges & Tags

- **Geometry:** Height-compact, fully pill-shaped (`100px` radius), padding `4px 11px`, typography `11px`, weight 600.
- **Variants:** Semantic combinations defined in the Color section (`ok`, `pend`, `sky`, `bad`).

### 7. Multi-Role Navigation & Hero Banners

- **Banner Block:** Rounded `28px` container featuring a dark gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #3A5068, #1E2937 75%)`), containing an editorial serif greeting, quick search, and role switches.
- **Sidebar Integration:** Fixed navigation links use pill contours (`11px 16px`), transitioning to the teal gradient when active.
